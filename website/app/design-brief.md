# Design brief: Why I Work Safely (AtkinsRéalis Safety Week 2026)

## Design read
An internal campaign site for the AtkinsRéalis PMS team on the O&M of Riyadh Metro, Safety Week 4 to 8 October 2026. Register: proud, warm, serious about people, never preachy.

## Concept spine
"The journey home." Every safe choice at work is a step on the way back to the people waiting for us. The film starts on a hard hat left on a platform railing at the end of a shift and rises over Riyadh until the metro line becomes a glowing ribbon leading home.

## Delivery tier
cinema

## Locked palette (user brand, taken from the Safety Week deck)
- Navy #192D38 (AtkinsRéalis brand navy, primary ground)
- Deep navy #0E1B23 (film ground, footer)
- Magenta #BE02F8 (campaign colour: hard hats, vests, icons)
- Lime #C8FF00 (the "Safely" lettering on the campaign badge; used sparingly as signal)
- Steel #B7D0E3 and Mist #E2ECF4 (deck secondary tints)
- Paper #F3F7FA (light sections)
Defense: these are the user's own campaign and corporate colours, which override the generic palette bans.

## Locked type
Space Grotesk (display, echoes the geometric campaign lettering) + Manrope (body).

Animation mode: animated-website

## Journey
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous 15s crane rise, split at frame-exact cut points into three chapters (consecutive frames of one take, so every seam is pixel identical).
1. Hard hat on the railing at blue hour. "Someone is waiting for you to come home today."
2. Camera rises as the metro glides in. "Safety is a promise, not a rule." Tags: My family, My colleagues, Myself.
3. Aerial over the city, the line a magenta ribbon home. "Safety is a choice I make every day."

World grammar: photoreal, blue hour, deep navy sky, magenta light strips, warm amber home lights, slow steady crane, no text.
Journey enacts spine: the camera literally travels from the workplace to the homes.
Mobile framing: subject center-safe; mobile encode capped at 720p.
Delivery budget: desktop under 32 MiB, mobile under 16 MiB.

## Section plan
1. Journey (scroll-scrub film, 3 chapters)
2. Welcome: split layout, three briefing tiles + crew illustration
3. Why I work safely: oversized statement + three-column triad
4. From compliance to commitment: ascending staircase (Bradley curve)
5. Five habits: numbered editorial rows
6. Take 5: interactive five-step selector
7. Near misses matter: proportional pyramid
8. Stop work authority: full-bleed magenta band, two lists + illustration
9. Pledge wall: live form + wall of signed pledges (D1)
10. Ask yourself: four reflection cards
11. The week: five-day timeline with today's marker
12. Key message band + footer

## Asset plan
Film (Higgsfield Kling 3.0) + posters; deck illustrations (crew, team), campaign badge, AtkinsRéalis logo (white + navy), Riyadh Metro train strip, deck icon set (masked to brand colours); launch cover + OG.
